Both give you a LuminPay-hosted page to share by WhatsApp, email, SMS or social media. The customer picks a coin and network, pays, and you're settled in naira. You can create them in the dashboard or with the API.

| | Payment link | Invoice |
|---|---|---|
| Use it for | A product, service or donation, paid by anyone, any number of times | One bill to one named customer |
| Amount | Fixed, or the customer types it | Fixed |
| Paid | Every time someone pays | Once |
| Hosted page | `https://app.luminpay.co/l/{slug}` | `https://app.luminpay.co/invoice/{reference}` |
| Fee | Included in your price; the customer pays exactly what you set | Same |

In Sandbox the pages are on `sandbox.luminpay.co`, and customers pay with [test coins](/guides/networks#getting-test-coins).

## Create a payment link

```bash
curl -X POST "$LUMINPAY_BASE_URL/payment-links" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LUMINPAY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "title": "Haircut and beard trim",
    "amountType": "fixed",
    "fixedAmountNgn": "5000",
    "successRedirectUrl": "https://example.com/thanks"
  }'
```

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| `title` | Shown to the customer. Up to 120 characters. |
| `description` | Optional. Up to 1,000 characters. |
| `amountType` | `fixed`, or `flexible` to let the customer enter the amount. |
| `fixedAmountNgn` | Whole naira, required for `fixed`: `"5000"` is ₦5,000. |
| `successRedirectUrl` | Optional. Where to send the customer after paying. Must start with `https://` on Live. |

The response includes `url`; that's the link to share. `fixedAmountNgn` comes back in kobo (`"500000"`), like every naira amount the API returns.

Turn a link off with `PATCH /payment-links/{id}/status` and `{"status": "inactive"}`; customers then see that it's no longer active. See every payment made through a link with `GET /payment-links/{id}/charges`.

## Create an invoice

```bash
curl -X POST "$LUMINPAY_BASE_URL/invoices" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LUMINPAY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "customerName": "Ada Obi",
    "customerEmail": "ada@example.com",
    "description": "Website design, October",
    "amountNgn": "250000",
    "dueDate": "2026-11-30"
  }'
```

`amountNgn` is whole naira. Send the returned `url` to your customer. The customer's email is never shown on the public page.

- An invoice can be paid once. Its `status` goes from `sent` to `paid`.
- Each time the customer opens it, they get 15 minutes to pay the quoted amount. If they come back later, a fresh quote is made automatically.
- A payment for less than the full amount leaves the invoice open; you'll see what arrived in the dashboard.
- Cancel an unpaid invoice with `PATCH /invoices/{id}/void`.

## Know when you're paid

Listen for webhooks:

- `charge.paid` for every payment. Its `data` includes `paymentLinkId` or `invoiceId`, so you can tell which link or invoice it came through.
- `invoice.paid` when an invoice is settled, with `invoiceId` and `reference`.

See [Webhooks](/guides/webhooks) for verifying them.
