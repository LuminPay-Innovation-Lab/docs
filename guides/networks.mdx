## Getting test coins

| Network | Test coins | Gas |
|---|---|---|
| Ethereum Sepolia | USDC from [faucet.circle.com](https://faucet.circle.com) (choose Ethereum Sepolia) | Sepolia ETH from any Sepolia faucet |
| Solana devnet | USDC from [faucet.circle.com](https://faucet.circle.com) (choose Solana Devnet) | Devnet SOL from [faucet.solana.com](https://faucet.solana.com) |
| BNB Smart Chain testnet | USDT from the [BNB Chain testnet faucet](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/testnet-faucet) | tBNB from the same faucet |
| Bitcoin testnet3 | Testnet BTC from any testnet3 faucet | — |

Check that the token contract in your wallet matches the table above. Test tokens with the same name on a different contract won't be detected.

You can watch test payments on [sepolia.etherscan.io](https://sepolia.etherscan.io), [testnet.bscscan.com](https://testnet.bscscan.com), [explorer.solana.com](https://explorer.solana.com/?cluster=devnet) (devnet) and [mempool.space/testnet](https://mempool.space/testnet).

## Confirmation times

Stablecoin payments usually confirm within a minute. Bitcoin needs one block confirmation, about 10 minutes on average, and sometimes longer. Every network is checked at least every two minutes, so `charge.paid` arrives within a couple of minutes of confirmation.
