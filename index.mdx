LuminPay lets your customers pay with USDT, USDC or Bitcoin while you receive naira in your bank account. This documentation covers the API for building payments into your own website, app or platform.

<Tip>
  New here? The [Quickstart](/quickstart) takes you to a paid test charge in about ten minutes.
</Tip>
